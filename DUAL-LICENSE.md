# Bond Dual Licensing at a Glance

> **Draft — not legal advice; requires attorney review before reliance.**

Summary only. The binding texts are [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (AGPLv3) and the signed Institutional Commercial License agreement. The policy behind this table is in [`LICENSE-POLICY.md`](LICENSE-POLICY.md).

| | **AGPLv3 (open source)** | **Institutional Commercial** |
|---|---|---|
| Intended for | Individuals, couples, self-hosters, researchers | Clinics, therapists, group practices, treatment centers, other organisations serving clients |
| Cost | Free | Paid: Private Practice $49/mo ($39/mo annual, 10 seats), Clinical Group $199/mo ($159/mo annual, 50 seats), Treatment Center $499/mo ($399/mo annual, 150 seats), Enterprise by contract; extra seats $5 / $4 / $3 per seat per month |
| Use, study, modify | Yes | Yes |
| Offer a modified version to others over a network | Only if you publish the full modified source under the AGPLv3 | Yes, modifications may stay private |
| Combine with proprietary software (EHR, practice management) | Combined work falls under AGPLv3 terms | Permitted under the agreement's terms |
| Bond-hosted clinician dashboard and institutional plans | Not included | Included, per seat tier |
| Support / SLA | None | Per agreement |
| Warranty and indemnity | None (AGPLv3 sections 15–16) | Per agreement |
| Business Associate Agreement (HIPAA) | Not available | Can be negotiated; HIPAA compliance is **not** established today |
| Bond name and logo | No trademark rights | As granted in the agreement |
| Contributing back | Welcome, under the [CLA](CLA.md) | Welcome, under the [CLA](CLA.md) |

An organisation may use the AGPLv3 edition if it fully complies with the AGPLv3. A commercial license is required for the hosted institutional service, or to use Bond without meeting AGPLv3 obligations.
