# BOND - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 26, 2025**

## Introduction

Welcome to BOND ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using BOND, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information You Provide

When you register for and use our App, we collect:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password (encrypted)
- **Relationship Information:** Relationship stage, duration, goals
- **Assessment Responses:** Your answers to relationship assessments
- **Check-in Data:** Daily mood, connection scores, appreciation notes
- **Messages:** Communications with your partner within the app
- **Profile Data:** Profile pictures (if uploaded)

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Features used, time spent, navigation patterns
- **Log Data:** IP address, app crashes, performance data
- **Push Notification Tokens:** To send you notifications

### 3. Information from Third Parties

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

- **Supabase:** Database and authentication services
- **OpenAI:** AI-powered insights generation (anonymous processing)
- **Expo:** Mobile app infrastructure and push notifications

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide Services:**
   - Create and manage your account
   - Process assessment responses
   - Generate personalized AI insights
   - Enable partner communication
   - Track your relationship progress

2. **Improve Experience:**
   - Analyze usage patterns
   - Fix bugs and improve performance
   - Develop new features
   - Personalize content

3. **Communication:**
   - Send daily check-in reminders
   - Notify you of partner activity
   - Provide customer support
   - Send important updates

4. **Security:**
   - Prevent fraud and abuse
   - Protect user data
   - Enforce our Terms of Service

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Share Your Data With:

1. **Your Partner:** When you connect with a partner, they can see:
   - Your assessment results (after both complete)
   - Your messages to them
   - Your daily check-ins (if you choose to share)

2. **Service Providers:**
   - **Supabase:** Secure database hosting (US-based, SOC 2 compliant)
   - **OpenAI:** Anonymous AI processing (no personal identifiers sent)
   - **Expo:** Push notifications and app updates

3. **Legal Requirements:**
   - If required by law, court order, or government request
   - To protect our rights, safety, or property
   - In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

### We DO NOT:
- ❌ Sell your personal information
- ❌ Share your data with advertisers
- ❌ Use your assessment responses for marketing
- ❌ Share your relationship data publicly

---

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

1. **Encryption:**
   - All data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL)
   - Passwords hashed with bcrypt
   - Assessment answers encrypted at rest

2. **Access Control:**
   - Row-level security in database
   - Authentication required for all actions
   - Partner data isolation

3. **Infrastructure:**
   - Secure cloud hosting (Supabase)
   - Regular security audits
   - Automated backups

**However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.**
