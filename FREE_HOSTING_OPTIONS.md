# Free Backend Deployment Options for BOND

## Comparison of Free Hosting Platforms

| Platform | Free Tier | Always-On | Cold Start | Best For |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Render** | 750 hrs/mo | No (spins down) | ~30s | Easiest setup |
| **Fly.io** | 3 VMs free | Yes | None | Best performance |
| **PythonAnywhere** | Forever free | Yes | None | Python-specific |
| **Deta Space** | Unlimited free | Yes | ~1-2s | Completely free |
| **Koyeb** | Limited free | No | ~15s | Modern platform |

---

## Recommended: Render.com

**Why Render:**
- ✅ Easiest deployment (1-click from GitHub)
- ✅ 750 hours/month free (enough for always-on)
- ✅ Automatic SSL
- ✅ Built-in CI/CD
- ✅ No credit card required

**Drawbacks:**
- Spins down after 15 min inactivity (cold start ~30s)
- Limited to 750 hours/month

---

## Alternative 1: Fly.io

**Why Fly.io:**
- ✅ 3 shared VMs completely free
- ✅ Always-on (no cold starts)
- ✅ 3GB persistent storage
- ✅ Better performance
- ✅ Global deployment

**Drawbacks:**
- Requires credit card (but won't charge)
- More complex setup

---

## Alternative 2: PythonAnywhere

**Why PythonAnywhere:**
- ✅ Forever free tier
- ✅ Designed for Python apps
- ✅ Always-on
- ✅ No cold starts
- ✅ Simple setup

**Drawbacks:**
- Limited to 512MB RAM
- 1 web app only on free tier
- Daily quota limits

---

## Alternative 3: Deta Space

**Why Deta Space:**
- ✅ Completely unlimited free
- ✅ Modern micro-cloud platform
- ✅ No cold starts
- ✅ Built-in database options
- ✅ No credit card required

**Drawbacks:**
- Newer platform (less mature)
- Different deployment model
